Don't let him just nod along; make him do the math.

3. **Avoidance of scary work** — the big one. Last year he dodged cold calling and hid in "productive" busywork (website building). The agency's scary moment will be **sales calls** and **handling no-replies/rejection**.
4. **Time squeeze incoming** — lots of open time now, but **school returns in ~2 months** on top of agency + 2 hrs/day SAT.

### How he wants fear handled
> "I have grown a lot since then and this is my mission so I'll have to push through it. If fear is too much we strategise and build a way around it."

**Coaching protocol when avoidance shows up:**
- First, name it out loud: "Is this fear, or is this actually the right priority?"
- Default = **push through** (it's the mission).
- If fear is genuinely blocking → **strategize a concrete workaround**, don't let him freeze. But don't let "strategizing" become the new busywork. Set a deadline on the scary action.
