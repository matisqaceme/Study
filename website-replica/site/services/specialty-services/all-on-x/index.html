Full-Arch Tooth Replacement on as Few as Four Implants

All-On-X Dental Implants in Summit, NJ

All-On-X is a full-arch dental implant solution that provides fixed, natural-looking teeth with fewer implants and faster recovery.

All-On-X dental implants are a full-arch tooth replacement option for patients missing most or all of their upper or lower teeth. At Prism Oral Surgery & Implants of Summit, Dr. Jean Kim provides All-On-X implant surgery in Summit, NJ for patients from Summit, Chatham, Madison, Millburn, Maplewood, and nearby Union County communities who want a fixed alternative to removable dentures.

All-On-X uses a carefully planned number of dental implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth. For many patients, this approach can improve chewing function, stability, speech, and confidence while reducing the need for an implant at every missing tooth site.

What Is All-On-X?

All-On-X, sometimes referred to as All-On-4 or All-On-6, is a full-arch dental implant treatment that uses strategically positioned implants to support a complete set of prosthetic teeth. Instead of replacing each missing tooth with a separate implant, this method typically uses 4 to 6 implants per arch.

The implants are placed at specific angles and locations to make use of available jawbone. In some cases, this can reduce the need for additional procedures such as bone grafting, although every patient must be evaluated individually.

Patients considering full-arch implant treatment may also benefit from learning more about dental implants and how they support long-term tooth replacement planning.

Benefits of All-On-X Dental Implants

All-On-X may offer several benefits for patients in Summit, NJ and surrounding communities who are looking for a stable full-arch tooth replacement option.

Benefits may include:

  • Fixed replacement teeth that are not removed daily by the patient
  • Improved chewing stability compared with traditional removable dentures
  • A more secure fit with less slipping or movement
  • Support for speech, comfort, and confidence
  • Fewer implants than replacing each tooth individually
  • Immediate temporary teeth in select cases
  • A long-term solution when properly maintained

All-On-X can be a helpful option for patients who want a full-mouth solution but do not want traditional dentures as their primary long-term treatment.

Who Is a Candidate for All-On-X?

You may be a candidate for All-On-X dental implants if:

  • You have lost most or all of your upper or lower teeth
  • You currently wear removable dentures and want a more stable option
  • You have failing teeth that may need to be removed
  • You want fixed teeth that feel more secure during daily function
  • You have enough bone volume or anatomy that can support angled implant placement
  • You are healthy enough for oral surgery
  • You are willing to follow post-operative instructions and long-term maintenance recommendations

Dr. Kim will perform a comprehensive consultation at Prism Oral Surgery & Implants of Summit using advanced diagnostic imaging. This helps evaluate bone quality, oral health, medical history, bite function, and whether All-On-X is appropriate for your needs.

Patients from Chatham, Madison, Millburn, Maplewood, Short Hills, Springfield, New Providence, and Berkeley Heights often visit the Summit office for implant consultations and specialty oral surgical care.

Who May Not Be a Good Candidate?

All-On-X is not right for every patient. You may not be an ideal candidate if:

  • You have uncontrolled medical conditions that affect healing
  • You smoke heavily and are not willing to reduce risk factors
  • You have active gum disease or untreated oral infection
  • You do not have adequate bone support for implant placement
  • You are unable to maintain routine oral hygiene and follow-up visits
  • You need a different type of tooth replacement based on your anatomy or goals

Some patients may need preparatory treatment before implant placement. Depending on your evaluation, Dr. Kim may discuss procedures such as bone grafting or ridge improvement before moving forward with full-arch implant treatment.

Our All-On-X Process in Summit, NJ

1. Consultation and Digital Planning

Your treatment begins with a detailed consultation at Prism Oral Surgery & Implants of Summit. Dr. Kim reviews your medical history, dental concerns, smile goals, and existing oral health.

Digital imaging may be used to evaluate bone structure and plan implant positions. Dr. Kim also coordinates with your restorative dentist or prosthodontist to help plan the final prosthetic teeth.

2. Treatment Coordination

All-On-X treatment often involves both a surgical phase and a restorative phase. Dr. Kim focuses on the surgical implant placement, while your restorative dentist designs and delivers the temporary and final prosthetic teeth.

This team-based approach helps ensure that the implants and final restoration are planned together.

3. Surgical Implant Placement

On the day of surgery, 4 to 6 implants are typically placed in the planned locations. Sedation options may be available depending on your health, procedure plan, and comfort needs.

For patients who feel anxious about oral surgery, Prism Oral Surgery offers supportive options such as IV sedation when clinically appropriate.

4. Temporary or Staged Teeth

Many patients may receive a temporary set of fixed teeth on the same day as surgery. This is sometimes called “teeth in a day.” However, same-day teeth are not guaranteed for every case and depend on bone quality, implant stability, and the treatment plan.

Final restorations are typically placed after the implants have healed and integrated with the jawbone.

5. Healing and Long-Term Follow-Up

Healing often takes several months. During this period, Dr. Kim monitors your recovery and coordinates with your restoring dentist.

Long-term success depends on home care, professional maintenance, bite stability, and routine dental visits.

Timeline for All-On-X Treatment

The full All-On-X process usually takes several months from consultation to final teeth.

A general timeline may include:

  • Initial consultation and imaging
  • Surgical planning with the restorative dentist
  • Implant placement surgery
  • Temporary teeth, when appropriate
  • Healing and implant integration
  • Final prosthetic restoration
  • Ongoing maintenance visits

Many patients receive temporary teeth on the day of surgery, but final teeth are typically completed after healing, often within 3 to 6 months depending on the case.

Cost Factors for All-On-X in Summit, NJ

The cost of All-On-X dental implants can vary because treatment is customized for each patient.

Common cost factors include:

  • Whether one arch or both arches are treated
  • The number of implants needed
  • Whether teeth need to be extracted
  • The need for bone grafting or related procedures
  • Type of sedation used
  • Complexity of surgical planning
  • Temporary and final prosthetic design
  • Coordination with the restorative dentist

During your consultation, the team at Prism Oral Surgery & Implants of Summit can review your surgical treatment plan and help explain expected costs. The practice accepts several insurance providers and can discuss available payment details based on your treatment needs.

Risks and Limitations

All-On-X is a well-established treatment option, but like any oral surgical procedure, it has risks and limitations.

Possible risks may include:

  • Swelling, bruising, or discomfort after surgery
  • Infection
  • Delayed healing
  • Implant failure
  • Bone loss around implants
  • Prosthetic wear or maintenance needs
  • Need for additional procedures
  • Changes to speech or bite during adaptation

Dr. Kim will review risks, benefits, alternatives, and expected recovery before treatment. A careful consultation is important so patients understand what All-On-X can and cannot accomplish.

All-On-X vs. Traditional Dentures

All-On-X and traditional dentures both replace a full arch of missing teeth, but they work differently.

Traditional dentures rest on the gums and are removed by the patient. They may move during eating or speaking, and they often require adhesive or periodic adjustment.

All-On-X uses implants placed in the jawbone to support fixed teeth. This can provide greater stability and chewing support than removable dentures for many patients.

Patients who are deciding between fixed implant teeth and removable options may also want to review implant supported dentures to better understand the differences between treatment approaches.

Alternatives to All-On-X

All-On-X is one option for full-arch tooth replacement, but it is not the only option.

Alternatives may include:

  • Traditional removable dentures
  • Implant supported dentures
  • Individual dental implants
  • Single tooth implants for isolated missing teeth
  • Bone grafting followed by staged implant placement
  • Other restorative plans recommended by your dentist

If you are missing one tooth rather than a full arch, a single tooth implant may be more appropriate than All-On-X.

FAQs

Is All-On-X the same as traditional dental implants?

No. Traditional dental implants may replace one tooth or several individual teeth. All-On-X uses a smaller number of implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth. This approach is often used for patients who are missing most or all teeth in the upper jaw, lower jaw, or both.

Can I get new teeth the same day as surgery?

Many patients can receive temporary fixed teeth on the day of surgery, but this depends on bone quality, implant stability, medical history, and the treatment plan. Dr. Kim will explain whether same-day temporary teeth are appropriate after your consultation and imaging.

How long do All-On-X implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last many years. The attached prosthetic teeth may need maintenance, repair, or replacement over time. Long-term success depends on oral hygiene, routine dental care, bite forces, medical health, and regular follow-up with your dental team.

Is All-On-X available near Chatham, Madison, Millburn, and Maplewood, NJ?

Yes. Prism Oral Surgery & Implants of Summit provides All-On-X dental implant surgery in Summit, NJ for patients from Chatham, Madison, Millburn, Maplewood, and surrounding communities. The office is located at 55 Woodland Avenue in Summit.

What if I have already been told I do not have enough bone?

You may still have options. All-On-X can sometimes use angled implant placement to work with available bone, but this depends on your anatomy. Dr. Kim may recommend imaging, bone grafting, sinus lift evaluation, or another treatment plan based on your specific needs.
