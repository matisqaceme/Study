Comprehensive Dental & Surgical Care in One Place

Services at Prism Oral Surgery & Implants of Summit

At Prism Oral Surgery & Implants of Summit, we offer a wide range of services — from routine cleanings to advanced oral surgery — all under one roof. Whether you need a single procedure or a full treatment plan, we’ll guide you with clarity, precision, and personalized care.

Our team is led by Dr. Jean Kim, DDS, a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon, and we’re equipped with state-of-the-art technology to deliver safe, efficient, and comfortable care for every patient.

What to Expect at Prism

Specialized Surgical Expertise
Dr. Jean Kim is trained in both medicine and dentistry, with extensive experience in oral and facial surgery.
Patient-Focused Sedation Options
We offer a full range of sedation, including IV sedation, oral conscious sedation, and general anesthesia for complex or high-anxiety cases.
Advanced Technology
From digital imaging to minimally invasive techniques, we use the latest tools to enhance precision and speed up healing.
Clear, Compassionate Communication
We take time to explain your options, answer questions, and make sure you feel informed and confident every step of the way.
